Essential ingredients for building AI agents

Key components involved in building ai agents:

* The environment, in which the AI agent interacts with

* Sensors, allowing the agent to perceive its environment

* The model, which helps the agent interpret its perception and guide its understanding

* Decision making logic, which determines how the agent selects appropriate actions.

* Actuators, the mechanisms actions and affects its environment.

* Feedback mechanisms allow the agent to evaluate whether it has successfully achieved its intended goals

**Environment – the external world that the agent perceives and interacts with

**Sensors – how the agent collects data about its surroundings

**Model – the agent’s way of making sense of information

**Decision-making logic – rules and objectives that guide the agent’s actions

**Actions – how the agent shapes the environment
